      The science

      Static or PNF holds of 30–60 s, 2–3 sets, spread over 5+ days a week, with about 10–20 min a week per area. Stretch to tight or mildly uncomfortable, never painful. Loaded slow lowering works as well as stretching (better with a real weight). Gains come mostly from tolerating the stretch better and fade if you stop. Stretching doesn't prevent injuries or speed recovery, and the app makes no such claims. Before runs: dynamic moves, no more than 60 s static per muscle. Variety is here to keep it interesting; the evidence doesn't say variety itself adds range.
